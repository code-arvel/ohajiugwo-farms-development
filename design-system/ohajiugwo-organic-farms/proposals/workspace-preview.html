PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION

Real work. Real texture.

Natural light, growing crops, honest agricultural context.

i
Proposed stock references, not photographs of this farm

These examples are for visual review. Authentic owner photographs take priority. Final selections, mobile crops, provenance and licences need review before they replace public images.

A distinct image for each story

Leafy greens

A fresh harvest, with recognisable leaves and natural texture. Avoid a generic macro image that hides the produce.

Seedlings

Young plants in a believable nursery or planting bed. Keep the details crisp and the background quiet.

About the farm

A separate documentary scene showing soil care or cultivation. Prefer an authentic owner photo; do not reuse the product image.

The approved landing-page sections, copy, logo and layout stay intact. These are candidate references, not final image replacements. External images need internet access; the rest of this proposal uses local assets.
